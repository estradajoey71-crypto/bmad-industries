What works today

Your games. New possibilities. One experimental fusion platform.

The flagship fusion is GTA V × Minecraft: both real games run at once and BMAD's bridge joins them. Everything below has been played live on our machine; nothing is a mock-up.

Your real Minecraft player in Los Santos

Minecraft runs underneath; GTA's controls drive your actual Minecraft player. Hotbar, inventory, hearts, hunger and XP are drawn inside GTA.

Mine, place, blow things up

Blocks you place become solid props in GTA's world. TNT and creepers damage GTA's cars and people; GTA's bullets, cars and fire hurt your Minecraft player.

Two modes, one key

F10 switches between MINECRAFT MODE and GTA MODE. In GTA mode, GTA's own character steps out where your Minecraft player stands: vehicles, weapons, GTA's camera. F10 again brings your Minecraft player to wherever you ended up.

Minecraft's creatures, GTA's AI

Mobs walk the streets with Minecraft's own AI. Light a Nether portal and the Nether invades: lava and fire on the streets, Nether creatures fighting Los Santos, until you close the portal.

Boss fight with the police

Summon the Wither over Los Santos and GTA's police engage it with their own AI; their bullets do Minecraft damage. A crossbow firework rocket is a GTA rocket that destroys the car it hits.

Missions and a creator, already in the app

Arcade's Fusions page has a Library, a Mix & Match picker (host + provider from the compatibility graph), built-in missions and a mission composer that counts the real events the games produce.

Honest limits

What it is not, yet.

  • Linux only, and only our desktop (Hyprland) is tested.
  • Not downloadable: host-game setup still needs developer scripts.
  • Some Minecraft mobs are still drawn as plain boxes; mobs can spawn inside GTA buildings.
  • GTA V Story Mode occasionally stalls on load (about 2 launches in 16); Arcade times out and tells you.
  • Last live acceptance of the flagship: 16 of 18 steps (2026-10-12). Nobody outside BMAD has played it yet.

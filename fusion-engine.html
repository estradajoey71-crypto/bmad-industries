Fusion Engine · research

Two games you own,
played as one.

The Fusion Engine connects two installed games through a versioned shared-memory bridge so one game's world hosts the other's player and mechanics.

Not available. The Fusion Engine is in development and is not included in BMAD Arcade or sold. It works with third-party games (Minecraft, Grand Theft Auto V, Raft, Over The Top: WWI) whose publishers' permissions for a commercial product have not been obtained. It is offline and single-player only and never touches anti-cheat or online services.

How it works

  1. Tell us about it by email (estradajoey71@gmail.com). Use the button for the service; it fills in the questions we need answered.
  2. We reply within 2 working days with a fixed written quote and our short service terms, or an honest no if the job does not fit our schedule or skills. Quotes are valid for 7 days.
  3. You accept and pay the invoice we send (card payment through Stripe). Nothing is charged before you accept a quote.
  4. We build and test it on your sample or content, then deliver the files with instructions. Delivery times are working days from when we have your content and payment.
  5. Revisions are included as listed. If we cannot deliver what the quote describes, you get your money back.

How we work: we build with modern tools, including AI coding assistants. Every delivery is run on your sample or checked in a browser, and reviewed by a person, before you receive it. Please send made-up or anonymized samples until a quote is accepted; we keep client files private and delete them 30 days after delivery unless you ask us to keep them.

You must own or have the right to use the text, logos, images and data you send. We do not give legal, tax or accounting advice. Any sales tax that applies to your project is shown on the quote.

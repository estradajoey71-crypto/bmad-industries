How it works

No surprises between the estimate and the invoice

  1. Tell us about the job

    Use the form or call. Photos help us bring the right tools to the first visit.

  2. On-site visit

    We measure, check for hidden problems, and answer questions. Usually 30–45 minutes.

  3. Written estimate

    Itemized labor and materials within two business days. Nothing starts until you approve it.

  4. Work & walkthrough

    Daily photo updates, then a final walkthrough together before the invoice.

Questions

Before you call

Do you charge for estimates?

No. On-site visits and written estimates are free within the service area.

Is there a minimum job size?

We book small repairs in half-day blocks, so a list of several small fixes is the most cost-effective way to use a visit.

Who buys the materials?

We can source materials and itemize them on the estimate, or install materials you have already bought.

What if you find a problem after work starts?

We stop, show you photos, and give you a written change estimate. Extra work only proceeds with your approval.

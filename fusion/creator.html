Fusion Creator

Pick a host world. Pick what lives in it.

The creator is where the universal fusion platform is heading. Below, what is in the app now and what is only planned, kept apart.

IN THE APP TODAY

What exists in the app today

  • Fusions → Library: every recipe with its status, the games it needs, the systems it links, SET UP (BMAD's own copy of your game, nothing of yours modified) and PLAY FUSION / STOP.
  • Fusions → Mix & Match: pick a host world and a provider from the compatibility graph. PLAY is offered only for a built recipe; otherwise it names the missing adapter and the next proof.
  • Fusions → Missions: built-in missions (Close Quarters, Mob Panic in Los Santos, Trench Raid, Boarding Party) and a composer; progress counts real events from the games.
  • A control router: one key (F10) cycles the control modes a recipe declares, with a timeout and fallback if a switch cannot complete.
  • A scripting layer (Luau) with a sandboxed Fusion API for tools, used today for game acquisition and checks.
PLANNED · NOT AVAILABLE

What the Fusion Creator is meant to become (PLANNED: not available)

  • Choose two compatible games and which one hosts the world.
  • Choose mechanics, abilities, creatures, weapons, items and environmental features to bring across, from a registry of what each adapter has proven.
  • Remap controls and camera per fusion.
  • Keep a persistent player profile (inventory, stats, abilities) with per-game translation and isolated saves.
  • Share configurations and your own mods with other players, within each game's rules.
  • AI-assisted adapter authoring as an optional developer aid only; the shipped product will never depend on per-session AI inference.

Any visual of the planned creator in our marketing is labelled "CONCEPT: not a feature".

Development roadmap

Three stages. No dates we cannot keep.

Each stage opens only when the one before it is true. Future stages are separate from what is shipped.

NOW

Marketing launch

  • This truthful landing page and gallery
  • Authentic gameplay demonstrations
  • Clearly stated compatibility
  • Founders waitlist (email)

Paid Fusion Alpha

  • Downloadable package with in-app host setup and uninstall
  • Verified install and launch path on clean machines

  • Save-data safety checks
  • Support, refunds, tax and legal readiness
  • Owner approval to take payments

BMAD ARCADE: FUSION ALPHA

Status: NOT YET DOWNLOADABLE

The Fusion engine runs on our reference machine and in our acceptance tests. It is not packaged for other computers yet: the host-game setup still uses developer scripts, it has only been tested on one Linux desktop, and we are checking the licensing questions before anyone pays for it. The waitlist is how you tell us you want it.

Flagship: GTA V × Minecraft

Verified in real gameplay

Your real Minecraft player in Los Santos

Minecraft runs underneath; GTA's controls drive your actual Minecraft player. Hotbar, inventory, hearts, hunger and XP are drawn inside GTA.

Mine, place, blow things up

Blocks you place become solid props in GTA's world. TNT and creepers damage GTA's cars and people; GTA's bullets, cars and fire hurt your Minecraft player.

Two modes, one key

F10 switches between MINECRAFT MODE and GTA MODE. In GTA mode, GTA's own character steps out where your Minecraft player stands: vehicles, weapons, GTA's camera. F10 again brings your Minecraft player to wherever you ended up.

Minecraft's creatures, GTA's AI

Mobs walk the streets with Minecraft's own AI. Light a Nether portal and the Nether invades: lava and fire on the streets, Nether creatures fighting Los Santos, until you close the portal.

Boss fight with the police

Summon the Wither over Los Santos and GTA's police engage it with their own AI; their bullets do Minecraft damage. A crossbow firework rocket is a GTA rocket that destroys the car it hits.

Missions and a creator, already in the app

Arcade's Fusions page has a Library, a Mix & Match picker (host + provider from the compatibility graph), built-in missions and a mission composer that counts the real events the games produce.

Known gaps
  • Linux only, and only our desktop (Hyprland) is tested.
  • Not downloadable: host-game setup still needs developer scripts.
  • Some Minecraft mobs are still drawn as plain boxes; mobs can spawn inside GTA buildings.
  • GTA V Story Mode occasionally stalls on load (about 2 launches in 16); Arcade times out and tells you.
  • Last live acceptance of the flagship: 16 of 18 steps (2026-10-12). Nobody outside BMAD has played it yet.

What a Fusion Alpha install will need (today's real requirements)

  • Linux x86_64. Tested only on Arch Linux with the Hyprland desktop; other desktops are untested (the session uses the desktop to find and place game windows). No Windows or macOS.
  • An NVIDIA GPU with at least 4.5 GB of free video memory and about 12 GB of RAM for GTA V + Minecraft.
  • Your own copies of the games: GTA V (Legacy edition, Story Mode; tested from the Epic Games Store through Heroic), Minecraft: Java Edition (your Microsoft account), Raft or Over The Top: WWI on Steam.
  • Script Hook V and the ASI Loader, downloaded by you from their author's site (freeware that may not be redistributed).
  • BMAD's own parts: the bridge, the GTA V script, the Minecraft mod and the Unity plugins. These are ours and are the only things we ship.
  • Nothing of yours is modified: Fusions run from separate BMAD profiles with their own prefixes and saves; your installs and saves stay untouched.

What must be true before we take money

  1. A downloadable package whose advertised minimum (GTA V × Minecraft: play, build, switch modes, stop cleanly) passes our automated acceptance on a clean machine, not just on ours.
  2. In-app setup for the host game (no developer scripts), with an uninstall that leaves your games exactly as they were.
  3. Documented supported game versions and a compatibility check before PLAY.
  4. Answers to the licensing questions around selling access to a Minecraft mod and a GTA V single-player script.
  5. Support, refunds (14 days), tax and legal pages approved by the owner.

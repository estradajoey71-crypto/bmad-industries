FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need to own the games?

Yes. BMAD Arcade never includes games, game files or assets. A fusion runs your own installed copies (for example GTA V from the Epic Games Store through Heroic and Minecraft: Java Edition with your Microsoft account) from separate BMAD profiles, so your installs and saves are untouched.

Which games work?

See the Supported games matrix. Today the flagship is GTA V × Minecraft, Raft × Minecraft also runs, and Over The Top: WWI × Minecraft runs with gaps. Everything else is experimental or planned. We do not claim every game is compatible.

Is this a mod, a remake, or two games at once?

Two real games at once, bridged. Minecraft runs for real underneath; GTA V shows your Minecraft player, blocks, mobs and explosions and sends its own world back. It is not a Minecraft-themed remake inside GTA, and not a GTA-themed Minecraft map.

Which operating systems?

Linux x86_64 only, tested on Arch Linux with the Hyprland desktop and an NVIDIA GPU. No Windows or macOS.

Can I download it now?

No. Nothing fusion-related is downloadable yet. The free BMAD Arcade launcher (public edition) is on the Download page; it does not include Fusions. Join the waitlist to be told when the alpha is ready.

Is it safe for my games and accounts?

Fusions are offline and single-player only. The GTA V script is inert in any online session and BMAD never runs GTA Online or touches anti-cheat. Your game installs, prefixes and saves are copied into separate BMAD profiles and never modified.

What does "experimental" mean here?

It ran live on our machine with disclosed gaps (listed per fusion). "Working" means verified in real gameplay on our machine with acceptance evidence. "Planned" means not available.

What about refunds, privacy and terms?

The 14-day refund policy, privacy policy and terms on this site apply to anything we sell. Nothing fusion-related is on sale yet.

Are you affiliated with Rockstar, Mojang or the other publishers?

No. BMAD Industries is an independent developer. See the statement at the bottom of every page.

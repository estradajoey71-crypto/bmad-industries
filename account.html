Your licence

There is no account to sign in to. After you buy, your licence file (bmad-arcade-license.json) arrives as an email attachment; keep that email.

Add it in BMAD Arcade under Settings → About & updates → Add licence file. It works offline and on every Early Access build. BMAD Arcade itself is always a free download.

Lost the file?

Write to estradajoey71@gmail.com from the email you bought with and we will send it again.

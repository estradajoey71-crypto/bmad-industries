Privacy Policy

Draft for review — BMAD Industries must have this reviewed by its own legal counsel before publication.

Effective 2026-10-08

The app

  • BMAD Arcade sends no telemetry or usage data. It has no BMAD account.
  • It reads your installed launchers' records (Steam, Heroic, Lutris, Prism Launcher) and your chosen library folders on your computer to show and launch your games. That information is not uploaded anywhere.
  • It contacts the internet only for these purposes, and never sends your library, files or identity:
  • Online indicator: while Arcade is open it checks once a minute whether a network is reachable by opening a connection to a public DNS service (1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8) without sending data.
  • Artwork for games in your library (Steam's and Epic's image servers, the libretro thumbnail service, Homebrew Hub).
  • Things you ask for: franchise details (Wikipedia), free/homebrew game downloads (Homebrew Hub, GitHub) and emulator cores (libretro buildbot).
  • Update checks against BMAD's signed release feed (a normal web request with no personal data).
  • Services you connect yourself, such as your own Jellyfin server.
  • Diagnostics are a file you create on purpose. It excludes your saves, library database, Vault, licence, account names, passwords and tokens. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share the file with support.
  • Your licence is a signed file on your computer; it is verified offline.

This website

  • No analytics or advertising scripts, no tracking cookies and no fingerprinting.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and the downloads on GitHub Releases. GitHub may keep standard server logs (IP address, time, file) for security and operations under its own privacy statement.
  • When BMAD's store server is running the site, it counts page views and campaign links (utm_source, utm_campaign) as anonymous daily totals. It stores no IP addresses and sets no cookies for this.
  • If you sign in to your account, a sign-in cookie keeps you signed in. It is used only for that.

Purchases

  • Payments are processed by Stripe, which receives the details needed to take the payment under its own privacy policy. BMAD receives your email address, the product, the amount, the order and licence identifiers and dates — never your card details.
  • Order confirmations and sign-in links are sent to your email address, through an email delivery provider when one is enabled (currently Resend).
  • Order records are kept as long as needed for your licence, refunds and tax and accounting obligations.

Your choices

  • You can delete the app's data at any time (uninstall with --purge, or remove its folders).
  • To see, correct or delete data BMAD holds about a purchase, contact support privately (not on public issue trackers); legally required accounting records may have to be kept.

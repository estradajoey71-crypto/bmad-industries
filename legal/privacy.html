Privacy Policy

Draft for review — BMAD Industries must have this reviewed by its own legal counsel before publication.

Effective 2026-10-11

The app

  • BMAD Arcade sends no telemetry or usage data. It has no BMAD account.
  • It reads your installed launchers' records (Steam, Heroic, Lutris, GOG offline installers) and the library folders you choose on your computer to show and launch your games. That information is not uploaded anywhere.
  • It contacts the internet only for these purposes, and never sends your library, files or identity:
  • Online indicator: while Arcade is open it checks once a minute whether a network is reachable by opening a connection to a public DNS service (1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8) without sending data.
  • Artwork for games in your library (Steam's and Epic's image servers, the libretro thumbnail service, Homebrew Hub).
  • Things you ask for: free/homebrew game downloads (Homebrew Hub, GitHub) and emulator cores (libretro buildbot).
  • Update checks against BMAD's signed release feed (a normal web request with no personal data).
  • Services you connect yourself, such as your own Jellyfin server.
  • Diagnostics are a file you create on purpose. It excludes your saves, library database, Vault, licence, account names, passwords and tokens. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share the file with support.
  • Your licence is a signed file on your computer; it is verified offline.

This website

  • No analytics or advertising scripts, no tracking cookies and no fingerprinting.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and the downloads on GitHub Releases. GitHub may keep standard server logs (IP address, time, file) for security and operations under its own privacy statement.
  • If you arrive through one of BMAD's own campaign links (utm_source, utm_campaign), the site keeps that label in your browser tab (session storage) until you close it. It is sent to Stripe only if you buy, so BMAD can tell which posts lead to purchases. There are no accounts or sign-in cookies.

Purchases

  • Payments are processed by Stripe on its own payment page, under its own privacy policy. BMAD receives your email address, the product, the amount, Stripe's processing fee, the order and licence identifiers, dates and the campaign label if any — never your card details.
  • Your licence email is sent from BMAD's Gmail account through Google, which handles the message under its own privacy policy; replies and support emails are kept in that mailbox. Stripe also emails you its receipt.
  • Order records (email address, order, licence identifier, amounts and dates) are kept in a database on BMAD's own computer, not in a third-party database, for as long as needed for your licence, refunds and tax and accounting obligations.
  • BMAD does not sell or rent personal information and does not use it for advertising.

Your choices

  • You can delete the app's data at any time (uninstall with --purge, or remove its folders).
  • To see, correct or delete data BMAD holds about a purchase, write to the support address on the Support page (not on public issue trackers); legally required accounting records may have to be kept.
  • BMAD Arcade and this site are not directed at children under 13.
  • Changes: when this policy changes, the effective date above changes; significant changes are also noted on this page. Because the site has no tracking, it does not respond differently to "Do Not Track" signals.

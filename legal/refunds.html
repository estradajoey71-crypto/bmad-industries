Refunds and Cancellations

Draft for review — confirm with BMAD Industries' payment provider terms and local consumer law before publication.

Effective 2026-10-11

BMAD Arcade Early Access licence (one-time purchase)

  • No subscription. The Early Access licence is a single payment. Nothing renews and nothing is charged again.
  • 14-day refund, no questions asked. Ask within 14 days of purchase by replying to your licence email or writing to the support address from the email you bought with. The refund goes back to the original payment method through Stripe; your bank decides how fast it appears (usually 5–10 business days).
  • After a refund the licence is withdrawn: please remove it from BMAD Arcade (Settings → About & updates → Remove licence). It is not sent again. The free version keeps working with all your games.
  • The free version stays free. BMAD Arcade without a licence keeps your library, launching, retro and Arcade Mode.

Always

  • Statutory consumer rights in your country apply in addition to this policy (in the EU and UK this 14-day refund is at least as generous as the legal right to withdraw from a digital purchase).
  • Questions about a charge? Write to us first; we will refund rather than have you go through a bank dispute.
  • Products marked "in development" are not sold and cannot be bought.

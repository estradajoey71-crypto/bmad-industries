Refunds and Cancellations

Draft for review — confirm with BMAD Industries' payment provider terms and local consumer law before publication.

Effective 2026-10-08

BMAD Arcade Early Access licence (one-time purchase)

  • No subscription. The Early Access licence is a single payment. Nothing renews and nothing is charged again.
  • 14-day refund, no questions asked. Ask within 14 days of purchase by replying to your order email or contacting support privately with your order number. The refund goes back to the original payment method through Stripe.
  • After a refund the order's licence download and account downloads are removed. Please remove the licence from BMAD Arcade (Settings → About & updates); the free version keeps working with all your games.
  • The free version stays free. BMAD Arcade without a licence keeps your library, launching, retro and Arcade Mode.

BMAD Docs Pro (monthly subscription, when it opens)

  • Cancel any time from Account → Manage billing. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for, then the account returns to the Free plan. Your documents stay available and exportable.
  • Refunds for a billing period are handled case by case through support; if a payment was taken in error, it is refunded in full.

Always

  • Statutory consumer rights in your country apply in addition to this policy.
  • Products in development (such as the Fusion Engine) are not sold and cannot be bought.
